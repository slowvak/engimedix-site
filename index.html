Core Capabilities

Practical Decision Support for Reading-Room and Protocoling Workflows

Generic EHR patient summaries often bury clinical details relevant to a specific imaging exam, while standard national guidelines do not always capture institutional scanner fleets or departmental protocol preferences. EngiMedix addresses both areas.

Exam-Specific Clinical Summaries

Extracts relevant clinical details—such as primary diagnosis, recent treatments, medications like corticosteroids, renal function (eGFR), and contrast allergies—tailored to the ordered exam and subspecialty rather than displaying a single generic patient summary.

Tailored to modality, clinical question & subspecialty

Protocol & Decision Support

Presents ranked protocol options (such as top 3 choices grounded in ACR Appropriateness Criteria and local departmental rules) alongside the supporting clinical facts from the chart so the clinician can review the basis and confirm or modify the selection.

Closed set of allowed protocols • Supporting facts displayed

Local Site Adaptation

Starts from standard clinical guidelines (such as ACR Appropriateness Criteria) and can be adapted to an institution's documented protocol preferences and scanner policies using controlled synthetic training examples, avoiding the retention of patient identifiers in model weights.

Controlled synthetic adaptation • No PHI in model weights

Standard Clinical Integration

Designed to connect via standard HL7 and FHIR interfaces to surface summaries and protocol options within existing departmental workflows—such as order comments or EHR/RIS sidebars—without requiring a separate standalone desktop application.

HL7 & FHIR R4 compatible • Minimal workflow disruption

Architecture & Governance

Engineered for Clinical Transparency and Hospital IT Standards

Our architecture prioritizes predictable deployment inside health system infrastructure, clear evidence attribution, and alignment with Clinical Decision Support principles.

01 • Standards-Based

HL7 & FHIR Integration

Reads order and clinical context via standard HL7 messages and FHIR R4 resources, surfacing output inside existing RIS/PACS order fields or EHR sidebars.

02 • Privacy-Preserving

Controlled Synthetic Tuning

Site adaptation uses controlled synthetic training examples shaped by local policy rules, avoiding the retention of patient identifiers inside model weights.

03 • Clinician Review

Clinical Decision Support

Designed as Clinical Decision Support (CDS) to display ranked options alongside supporting chart facts so clinicians can independently review and decide.

04 • Diagnostic Focus

Radiology & Pathology

Focused initially on diagnostic radiology protocoling and pre-read clinical synthesis, with planned expansion to anatomic pathology workflows.

Pilot Evaluation Framework

Measuring Operational Impact Without Disrupting Clinical Volume

Prospective departmental pilots begin in shadow mode, using existing RIS/PACS audit timestamps to establish an objective baseline before active workflow display.

M1

Protocol Turnaround Time

Tracks elapsed time from order entry to protocol completion using existing RIS audit logs across modalities and subspecialties.

M2

Pre-Dictation Review Time

Measures study-open to first-dictation latency on complex oncology and subspecialty exams where chart review is intensive.

M3

Protocol Concordance

Evaluates agreement between the department's finalized protocol and the ranked options presented, guiding local calibration.

M4

Regulatory Discipline

Built to operate within non-device Clinical Decision Support criteria today, while pursuing prospective validation and appropriate FDA pathways as scope expands.
